About Us

A Treasure Trove of Unique Finds

Step into The Wacky Rabbit and discover a world of whimsy and wonder. For over 25 years, we've been Myrtle Beach's go-to destination for unique gifts, charming home décor, and beach essentials. Whether you're hunting for the perfect souvenir or searching for a distinctive piece to brighten your home, we've got you covered.

Our friendly staff offers complimentary gift wrapping, and we ship nationwide so you can share the beach vibes with loved ones back home.
