Welcome to Our Shop!

A Myrtle Beach treasure for over 25 years

Step into The Wacky Rabbit and discover a world of whimsy and wonder! Located in the heart of Myrtle Beach, we're your go-to destination for unique gifts, charming home décor, and beach essentials.

Whether you're hunting for the perfect souvenir to remember your coastal vacation, searching for a distinctive piece to brighten your home, or picking up one of our ready-to-go picnic packs for a perfect beach day, we've got you covered.

Our friendly staff offers complimentary gift wrapping, and we ship nationwide so you can share the beach vibes with loved ones back home!
